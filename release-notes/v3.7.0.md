ClipMonster 3.7.0 lets you **find a moment in a game by describing it** ("red card after a VAR check", "technical foul on the bench"), makes **recap reels**, shows **where the opportunities are** in Research, and counts what your AI costs.

## New
- **Search by in-game event.** A search bar at the top of the **Sports** tab (and of **Research**, for the video you've selected). Type the moment: *red card or player ejection*, *missed penalty or controversial offside call*, *buzzer beater or game-tying shot in the final minute*, *manager yellow card or sideline argument*, *controversial pass interference in the 4th quarter*. ClipMonster reads the game's commentary in overlapping 15-second windows and compares it with your question by meaning and by the words commentators use for it. It also looks up the game's official play-by-play and places those events in the video. Each moment found is cut from about 12 seconds before to 25 after, on pauses between sentences, and joins the game's plays, ticked, ready for **Make clips** or **Make reel**. A moment that's only talked about ("that should have been a red") is marked, and scored lower. The same bar is in ClipMonster for Mac under *Your PC → Sports*, with the PC doing the work.
  - **Play-by-play feeds** (Settings → Sports): ESPN's public scoreboard needs no key; API-Football is used for soccer when you add its key; or give a JSON/CSV timeline you wrote. To place a full game's events, ClipMonster needs to know where a period starts: it hears it in the commentary ("the second half is under way"), reads the scoreboard's clock with Claude or Gemini, or you say so (`--anchor 2=58:40`).
  - **The sentence model** is all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (90 MB, downloaded the first time), running on this computer. Without it the search matches by words. Settings → Sports → Event search picks.
- **Recap reels.** **Make reel** on a game in the Sports tab turns its best 4 to 7 plays into one 65–85 s vertical video, long enough for Creator Rewards. It opens on a teaser of the best play, and a narrator reads the AI's two-sentence story of the game. The plays count down (*PLAY 4… NUMBER 1*), or run in the order they happened. The picture follows the action in 9:16. A score bug shows the score the broadcast's scoreboard showed (read by Claude or Gemini), with a lower-third naming each play's player. The crowd is lifted at every big moment, with the music ducked under the commentary, at -14 LUFS. From the command line: `ytclip sports-montage "GAME_URL_OR_FILE" --sport soccer --creator-rewards --output-dir "Videos/Sports"`.
  - New in reels: an order that tells the game as it happened and **holds the best play for the end**; **transitions** you choose (a mix, cross-dissolves, whip-pans or zooms, 0.3 s; Settings → Sports); lower-thirds that carry **the feed's own minute and scorer** ("90+4' WINNER · E. HAALAND") when the game has a play-by-play; and **Make reel of found**, a reel of the moments an event search found (every red card, each argued call).
- **Research: velocity, saturation and opportunity badges.**
  - **Velocity delta**: each video's views per hour against the median of its channel's other videos over the last 7 days (3x means it's breaking out). A channel ClipMonster hasn't seen enough of is measured against its category, and it says so.
  - **Saturation**: for the fastest risers, a YouTube search counts the Shorts other channels have already posted of it (2 a scan by default, 100 quota units each, at most 20 a day; Settings → Research).
  - **Badges** on the category pills and on each video: 🔥 *Hot Arbitrage* (breaking out, long-form, few short clips yet), 🔁 *High Retention* (clear rewatched moments), 🧊 *Oversaturated*, 📈 *Rising* (breaking out, saturation not checked).
- **Clip-Ability index for games.** After **Find highlights**, each game shows a 0-100 index: 30% momentum, 25% how peaked its Most replayed graph is, 20% crowd and commentary emotion, 15% controversy (what's said in the commentary), 10% how clear the commentary is over the noise. Hover it for the parts. A part that can't be measured is left out, not counted as zero.
- **One AI router, and what it costs.** `query_intelligence()` asks the local AI first and falls back to Claude or Gemini when the local one can't answer, takes too long, or you ask for deep reasoning. Every AI call in the app is now counted, by day and model, with its cost, in `ai_usage.json` in the app's folder. **Settings → AI** has a daily and a monthly spending limit: when one is reached the paid AIs pause and the local one carries on.

## Good to know
- TikTok and Instagram have no search ClipMonster is allowed to use, so saturation is measured on YouTube Shorts and stands in for them.
- ESPN's feed is public but undocumented, so it can change without notice; when it can't be read the search uses the commentary alone and says so. SofaScore and FotMob aren't used: they don't offer a public API and block automated reading.
- In a highlights cut there is no game clock to go by, so the feed's events are placed only where the commentary names them.
- The packaged Mac app doesn't run the event search itself; a linked PC does. From source on a Mac (`pip install -e ".[mac,search]"`) it runs with Core ML.
- League and broadcaster footage is usually claimed on TikTok (copyright) and can be muted or taken down. ClipMonster doesn't alter footage to get around that.

## Command line
- `ytclip sports-find "red card or player ejection" "GAME_URL_OR_FILE" --sport soccer`: the moments found, with where each is and why. Add `--feed espn:eng.1:401879244 --anchor 1=4:10 --anchor 2=1:06:40` for the official timeline, `--json` for scripts, `--reel` to make a reel of them.
- `ytclip sports-montage "GAME" --sport soccer --order climax --transitions dissolve`, and `--events "red card or controversial call"` for a reel of found moments.
- `ytclip ai ask "…" [--priority local_first|cloud_first|cloud_deep|local_only|cloud_only] [--schema file.json]`, `ytclip ai route`, `ytclip ai usage`, `ytclip ai hardware`.
