ClipMonster 2.9.0 finds new videos instead of the same ones, and shows clear status bars for everything it does.

## New
- **Status bars everywhere.**
  - **Automation tab.** A panel above the queue shows the video being clipped and its steps: Download, Analyse, Transcribe, Find clips, AI polish, Export and Quality check. Finished steps are ticked and the current one shows its own percent. There's a bar for the whole video with about how long is left. The estimate learns from how long each step took on the last video.
  - **Between videos.** The panel shows what automation is checking, such as the trending charts or a standing request's searches.
  - **Queue rows.** The working video's row has a bar too, and so does a video downloading early.
  - **Uploads.** The clip going up to Google Drive gets a bar.
  - **Studio.** Each video in the list shows its step (*Step 3/5 · Transcribe 42%*) and a bar with the time left. So does the status line at the bottom.
  - **Research and updates.** Research checks and update downloads show a percent and the time left.
  - **Downloads.** Downloads show their speed.
- **Fresh recommendations.** Running a request again now finds new videos instead of the same ones:
  - **Searches take turns.** A request keeps up to eight searches. Each run takes the next two, sorted a different way: best match, then newest, then most viewed. Without an AI key, ClipMonster adds other ways of saying the same thing ("funniest moments", "hilarious moments", "try not to laugh"…). With one, the AI writes the searches and writes new ones each week.
  - **Digs past repeats.** When a page of results is all videos you've seen or used, it reads the next page.
  - **Used videos stay out.** Videos already picked, queued, clipped or sent to the Studio are left out before anything is rated. That also saves AI calls.
  - **Repeats go lower.** Videos a request showed before keep their match, so the AI isn't asked about them again, and they're listed below the new ones.
  - **Standing requests** in automation work the same way. Its log says what was searched and how many matches were new.
  - **Find more.** On a request's results, this searches again and adds only videos that aren't in the list yet.
- **Research shows what's new.**
  - **Hide used** (on to start with) hides videos you've already used.
  - The new **Seen** column says when a video first showed up (**New** for the last day) or that it's **Used**.
  - **New only** shows just the new ones.
- **More videos for each niche.** Research also checks each region's chart for the categories your niches use, up to four, at 1 API unit each. Settings → Research → *Also check my niches' category charts*.
- **Creative Commons searches** sort a different way each time and keep what earlier checks found, so the list grows instead of repeating.

## Fixed
- **A pick that clipped nothing no longer uses up a slot.** Before, automation could "pick" a video that was already in its queue (one you queued by hand, say). It then queued nothing but still used up one of the day's picks, so fewer new videos were clipped. Videos already in the queue are now skipped like any other used video.
