ClipMonster 2.4 lets you ask for clips: a number of them, at a length, for a niche, with special requests in plain words. Each niche runs from the Automation tab, a morning report tells you what happened overnight, and automation now looks after itself while you sleep. Long moments come out as Part 1, 2 and 3, and clips no longer start or end part-way through a word.

## What's new

### Clip requests
**Automation → Requests → New request** (or `ytclip request`) asks for a number of clips, how long each should be, and which niche they're for:
- **From your links, or picked for you.** Paste the videos to clip, or let automation pick videos with your picking rules (this needs a YouTube Data API key).
- **Special requests.** Write what you want in plain words, such as "only the funniest reactions, end on the punchline". With an Anthropic key, the AI clip finder and the AI polish both follow them.
- **Progress.** The Requests page shows "4 of 10 ready" for each request. A request is done when enough clips have passed the quality check. It "finished short" when its links had no more good moments of that length.
- A request goes ahead of everything else in the queue, and its videos don't count against your daily limits. Its clips go to their own folder, `Automation\Requests\<date> <N clips a-b s>\`. A video you've clipped before is clipped again for a request.

### Niches, run from the Automation tab
- **Automation → Niches** shows each niche's picks today against its limit, its videos waiting and its clips today. You can pause or resume a niche there, press **Find videos now** to check its sources straight away, or request clips for it.
- A niche can set its own **clips per video** and **clip length**, over its style's.
- A niche's note is now called **Special requests**. With an Anthropic key, a niche or request with special requests uses the AI clip finder, so the requests are followed. This still counts towards **AI calls per day**.

### The overnight report
**Automation → Overnight report** shows what automation did while you were away, for the last 12 hours, 24 hours or 7 days:
- the clips made for each niche and each request;
- how many clips the quality check rejected, and the most common reasons;
- the videos that failed, and why;
- any pauses: YouTube sign-in requests, a full disk, or stuck work that was stopped.

The tab tells you when there's something new to read. `ytclip report` prints the same report.

### The health check
The **health check**, under the report, looks at everything automation needs:
- ffmpeg, and how old the downloader is;
- your YouTube and Anthropic keys (**Check now** tries them online);
- your folders, and the free space on their drives;
- whether TikTok Manager watches your Automation folder;
- niches with nothing to find videos with, and requests that can't be filled.

Each problem comes with the fix. Automation runs this check every time it starts. `ytclip doctor` runs it from the command line.

### Automation looks after itself
- **The disk can't fill up.** Before free space falls under **Keep this much disk free** (15 GB to start with), automation clears unfinished and finished downloads, previews and rejected clips. If that isn't enough, it pauses until there's room, then carries on by itself.
- **Stuck work is stopped.** An export that makes no progress for **Stop stuck work after** (30 minutes) is stopped and tried once more. If it gets stuck again, it's skipped and the reason is shown.
- **A crash doesn't lock it out.** After a crash or power cut, automation used to refuse to start for three minutes ("already running"). Now pressing Start takes over straight away.

### Part series
When a great moment runs longer than one clip, it carries on in **Part 2** (and Part 3):
- Each part starts on the next sentence, right where the last one stopped, and the series ends when the moment does.
- Every part except the last shows your "Follow for part 2" bottom text. If the quality check rejects a part, the part before it is made again without the promise.
- Parts count towards **Clips per video**, so a series takes the place of the weakest stand-alone clips. A weak last part never pushes out a better clip.
- The clip list marks the parts (P1, P2…). Each clip's `clips.json` record has `series`, `part` and `parts`, so TikTok Manager can post the parts in order.
- Set how many parts a series can have in **Find clips → Part series** (3 to start with). Set it to 1 to turn part series off.

### Clean cut points
- **No more cut-off words.** ClipMonster now listens to the audio around each clip's start and end. If an edge would land in the middle of a word, it moves into the nearest pause, preferring the pause between sentences.
- **Better cuts with auto-captions.** YouTube's auto-generated captions have no punctuation, so ClipMonster now uses the pauses it hears to tell where sentences end. In our test video of a fast talker with auto-captions, 2.3 cut 10 of 12 clip edges through a word. 2.4 cuts none.
- **Clips stay within your maximum length.** Rounding a clip out to a whole sentence could leave it a few seconds over **Max clip length**. Now it ends on the last sentence that fits.

## Good to know
- Measuring a video now keeps a finer loudness track for the cut points. Videos analysed by an older version have their audio measured again the first time you open them, which takes a moment.
- `ytclip request --link` takes several links at once (`--link A B C`), or `--link` more than once.

## Updating
Installed copies offer this update automatically. Close ClipMonster's other windows first. Automation pauses for the update and starts again afterwards.
