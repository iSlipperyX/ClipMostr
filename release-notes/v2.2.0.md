ClipMonster 2.2 can run the whole job without you. It finds videos you're allowed to clip, cuts and captions them, checks every clip, and learns from how your posted clips do. You still choose what to upload.

## What's new

### Picking rules
ClipMonster can now decide which videos to clip. The rules are under **Settings → Picking**, and the Research tab's **Pick** column shows what they would pick and why.
- **Where videos come from:** besides the trending charts, Research can follow a **channel list** (creators who allow clipping, each with a note saying why) and run **Creative Commons searches**.
- **Pick videos from:** your channel list and Creative Commons videos (the default), Creative Commons only, or anything that passes the rules.
- **The rules** skip videos that were already picked, are live, come from a blocked channel or category, have a blocked word in the title, are too short, too long or too old, or score too low. There are daily and per-channel limits. **Only pick videos in** keeps to the languages you choose (English by default).
- `ytclip research --picks` shows the picks on the command line.

### Automation
The new **Automation** tab runs everything in the background. It checks Research on your schedule, queues what the rules pick, and clips one video at a time. It keeps going after you close ClipMonster, and **Start with Windows** starts it each time you sign in.
- The queue survives restarts, and no video is ever queued twice.
- It's gentle with YouTube: one download at a time, 10 minutes apart. If YouTube asks the PC to prove it isn't a bot, downloads pause for 6 hours.
- **Recipes** give each category or channel its own style and view.
- **AI calls per day** keeps Claude costs predictable.
- On the command line: `ytclip auto`, plus `--once`, `--status`, `--add`, `--pause`, `--resume`, `--stop`, `--retry` and `--install`.

### AI polish
One Claude call per video:
- writes a **hook** for each clip, for the banner and your upload caption;
- fixes misheard names in the captions;
- **rates** each clip from 1 to 10;
- keeps the speaker in shot when cropping;
- leaves your captions off when the video already has subtitles burned in.

### Quality check
Every clip is watched once after it's made. Clips that are silent, black, frozen, the wrong length or rated too low by the AI move to a `_rejected` folder, so they never reach you as ready to upload. Clips that start or end mid-sentence are flagged. `ytclip qc FOLDER` checks old folders again.

### Learning from results
TikTok Manager 1.1 shares how your posted clips did. ClipMonster uses that to:
- rank channels whose clips do well higher, and ones that flop lower;
- show the AI polish which hooks worked;
- show **What's working** on the Automation tab, or with `ytclip results`.

It never changes your settings for you.

### Also new
- **A folder of second videos** for the stacked layout: each clip gets a different one.
- **Trim dead air:** clips start just before the first word instead of on silence.
- **Bleep strong swearing** in captions and `.srt` files (`--bleep`).
- `clips.json` records the video's licence, so TikTok Manager can add the credit line for Creative Commons videos.

## Fixed
- Captions from translated YouTube subtitles no longer show stray `&gt;&gt;` codes in captions, titles or file names.
- Commas and full stops no longer appear as caption words of their own.
- Words that YouTube's captions only showed once are no longer dropped.
- Captions stay up through short pauses instead of flashing off.
- The AI polish no longer writes hooks that run the clip down ("Nothing happens in this one"). Its criticism goes in the rating note instead.

## Updating
Installed copies offer this update automatically. Your settings, presets and library carry over. Automation doesn't run until you press **Start** on its tab.
