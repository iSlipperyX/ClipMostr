ClipMonster 2.2.1 fixes problems found by testing 2.2.0 on real videos.

## Fixed
- **Captions in the wrong language.** YouTube sometimes guesses a video's language wrong and offers, say, an Arabic transcript for an English interview, which made the captions, clip titles and file names Arabic. ClipMonster now prefers captions in the video's own language or English, and the log says which captions it used.
- **Right-to-left captions.** Arabic and Hebrew captions and banners now read right to left in the right order.
- **Swearing in automation.** Automation now bleeps strong swearing in captions (**Settings → Automation → Bleep strong swearing**, on by default).
- **Hidden Creative Commons options.** `ytclip research --picks` now lists videos from your channel list and Creative Commons searches before the trending charts, so you can see why they weren't picked even when nothing is.

## Changed
- **Age limit for picks.** Videos from your channel list or under Creative Commons can now be up to 14 days old (**Oldest channel-list or CC video** in Settings → Picking). Trending videos keep the 3-day limit. Before, almost no Creative Commons video was new enough to pick.

## Updating
Installed copies offer this update automatically. Your settings, presets and library carry over.
