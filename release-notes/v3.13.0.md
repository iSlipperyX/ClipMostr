ClipMonster 3.13.0 adds a **sports editor** to the Telegram Mini App and makes sports videos asked for from Telegram **yours to download**: they're no longer tied to a TikTok account and queued to post by themselves.

## New
- **Sports requests from Telegram are downloads.** A reel or clip asked for in the chat (`/sports`, `/reel`, `/games`, `/find`) or in the app (*Make → Sports highlights*) is made and kept on the PC, tied to no account, not queued, not scheduled. The request says so before it starts, and the result comes with **⬇ File**.
- **Download to your device.** *Videos → Downloads* in the app lists them. **⬇ Download** saves the full-quality file to the phone (Telegram's own download sheet on Telegram 8.0 and later, the browser's otherwise). **Send the file to our chat** has the bot send it as a file, which Telegram doesn't compress again; save it from the chat. A bot may send 50 MB, and a bigger file says so and points at Download.
- **Post it after all.** **📤 Queue it to post** on a download (the chat card, or *Post it after all* in the app, where you pick the category) puts it in that category's account's queue like any other video. To have a request queued from the start, say *post it* in the chat, or pick **Queue it to post** in the app's form. Requests made on the PC's Sports tab and from the Mac app queue as before.
- **The sports editor.** **✂️ Edit** on a sports reel or clip (in the app, or the button under its card in the chat) opens an editor:
  - *A reel*: trim each play against the game's own footage (0.2 s and 1 s nudges, sliders, *Start here* / *End here* at the point the footage is playing), reorder, take plays out, add from the game's other plays and found moments, change each play's label and lower third, turn the opening teaser on or off, rewrite its headline and the narrator's words, pick another voice or none, set the music (off, quiet, normal, loud) and the game's own sound, follow the action or show the whole picture, pick the transitions, turn the score bug off, and rewrite the final score line, caption and hashtags.
  - *A single clip*: cut it again (3 to 180 s), add a headline, change the caption and hashtags, show the whole picture or fill the screen and choose which part, and turn the captions on or off.
  - **Make this version** has the PC cut the footage again. The result comes back as **v2 (Edited)** beside the first: both stay to download, and *Keep only v2* (or v1) deletes the other. An edit of an edit is v3, starting from how v2 was made.
  - Both people who share the bot see who edited what, and the edit's progress shows in the one progress message.

## Changed
- The minimums a Telegram request is checked against (over a minute, 9:16, sound) apply to videos made **to post**. A download has none to meet, so a 20-second single play is simply handed over.
- A reel's plan now records how it was made (voice, framing, music, game sound, score bug), so an edit starts from the real settings.

## Good to know
- **The preview is the footage, not the finished video.** The editor plays the game's video as the PC downloaded it: uncropped, with no graphics, music or narrator. The framing, mix and titles are made when the PC cuts the version.
- **When the footage has left the PC's cache** (the PC clears old downloads), the editor still works by the times, and the PC downloads the game again to make the edit.
- **Reels made before 3.13 can be edited** when their folder still has its `reel.json`; their voice and framing are assumed to be the defaults.
- A posted video, or one going up right now, can't be edited. Only one edit of a video is made at a time.
- A narrator's voice is synthetic, so a reel with one is labelled AI-generated if it's ever posted.
- League footage often gets copyright claims on TikTok.
