ClipMonster 3.5.0 puts ClipMonster on your phone and lets the Video Creator run by itself: a Telegram bot of your own to ask for videos, watch what's waiting to post and delete the bad ones from the PC, Creator videos made for each category every day, a weekly report on Monday mornings, TikTok covers picked from the clip, faster Creator rendering on the graphics card and a much smaller download.

## New
- **ClipMonster on your phone (Telegram).** Make a bot with Telegram's @BotFather, paste its token in *Settings → Telegram*, press **Link a phone** and send the bot the code it shows. While automation runs, the bot answers that phone (and only phones you link):
  - **🎬 Videos**: everything waiting to post, held ones first. Tap one to watch the video itself, then **✅ OK to post**, **🚀 Post it now** or **🗑 Delete**, which asks first and then deletes it from the PC the way Clean up clears a posted clip (the Recycle Bin, if *Settings → Posting* says so). Automation never brings it back.
  - **✨ Make a video**: `/make the real story of the Bermuda Triangle`, pick the category, and the Creator video comes back to the chat when it's made. **✂️ Clip a link**: send a YouTube link and the clips come back when they're done. **📋 Requests**: `/request 10 30-45s Gaming funny reactions only`, in plain words.
  - **📊 Status** (what automation is doing, what's waiting, the next post, free space), which **📌 Keep this up to date** pins as a live dashboard, **⏯ Pause or resume**, and **📅 This week**.
  - By itself, as *Send me* says: each video that waits for your OK (with the video), new clips queued to post, each post as it goes up, milestones, problems that need you (a TikTok sign-in running out, a post failing, YouTube asking the PC to sign in, low disk space) and the weekly report.
  - A video over Telegram's 50 MB bot limit goes as a smaller 720p copy; what posts is always the full-quality file. Videos upload in the background, so the bot keeps answering, and a video's message changes when it's OK'd, posted or deleted somewhere else. Use a bot just for ClipMonster: Telegram lets one program at a time read a bot's messages.
- **Creator videos every day.** In the Categories tab, give a category a number of **Creator videos** a day (up to 6) and, if you like, its own **Topics**, one idea per line. Automation plans them once a day, taking turns between your topics (each used at most once a month) and what's trending in the category on YouTube right now (phrases the newest videos share, like "GTA 6", looked up online so the script covers today's news on it, never someone's video), and makes them overnight from 1 am so they're ready in the morning (*Settings → Creator → Make the daily videos*: overnight, or straight away).
- **Weekly report.** At 9 am on Mondays a notification sums up each TikTok account's week: views against the week before, how many were on videos over a minute (the ones Creator Rewards pays for), followers, the best and weakest post, older videos that took off, clips against Creator videos, then what automation made and what waits for you. **Performance → Weekly report** shows it any time, it goes to your phone when the bot is set up, and `ytclip report --week` prints it.
- **TikTok covers.** Each post's cover is picked from the clip instead of TikTok's first frame (a dark frame with half a headline, for clips that open on a hook card): the moment the hook card is fully up, else the clearest, biggest face, else the clearest frame. It's set in TikTok Studio's cover editor just before Post, and the Queue tab shows it. If TikTok's editor doesn't take it, the post goes up anyway with TikTok's pick. *Settings → Posting → TikTok cover*.
- **Creator videos clean up after themselves.** Once a Creator video posts, its folder goes like a clip's: the scene pictures and storyboard too. Its posted record keeps the prompt, the script, the voice, where the pictures came from, the sources and the fact check.

## Faster
- **Creator rendering.** Scenes are animated several at a time (one at a time with *Settings → Speed → Quiet*) and encoded on the graphics card when ffmpeg can use it (AMF on your Radeon), near-lossless, before the final encode. If the card's encoder fails on a scene, that scene is redone on the processor.
- **A smaller download.** The app leaves out files that come with its libraries but are never loaded (dictionaries for languages no voice speaks, OpenCV's own FFmpeg, Qt's touch keyboard and translations, and more), and the update zip is packed with LZMA. Updates now download 203 MB instead of 412 MB, and `ClipMonster-Setup.exe` is 183 MB instead of 290 MB. Every build now checks that each library it needs still loads before it's published.

## Fixed
- The British voices (Calm Documentary and British Male) now sound British: Kokoro was reading them with American pronunciations.

## Good to know
- Windows' *Extract all* can't open the new update zip (LZMA). ClipMonster's updater unpacks it itself; to install by hand, run `ClipMonster-Setup.exe`, or open the zip with 7-Zip.

## Command line
- `ytclip report --week` prints the weekly report (`--json` for scripts).
- `ytclip self-test` checks that every library this install needs loads.
- `ytclip auto` runs the Telegram bot too, like automation started from the app.
