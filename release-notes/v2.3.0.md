ClipMonster 2.3 sorts automation's videos into niches, one for each TikTok account. It adds a Clean up window for everything TikTok Manager no longer needs, and it stops promising a "part 2" that doesn't exist.

## What's new

### Niches
The new **Niches** tab gives each of your TikTok accounts its own niche:
- **The account:** a name, the TikTok account it's for, how many videos a day it gets, and a switch to pause it.
- **Where its videos come from:** its own channel list and Creative Commons searches. Title words and YouTube categories catch videos from the charts.
- **How its clips look:** its own style, top/bottom view, second videos, and a note for the AI about what makes a good clip for that account.

Every picked video goes into one niche. Its clips go to that niche's own folder in the Automation folder, for example `Automation\Gaming\`. Each clip's `clips.json` records the niche and its TikTok account.

- **Fair shares:** each niche has its own daily limit. The overall limit (Settings → Picking) is shared out one pick at a time, and the niche with the fewest picks today goes first. This stops one busy niche from taking every pick.
- **A paused niche** keeps its own channels' videos back. They aren't handed to another account.
- The **Research** tab shows each video's niche and can filter by it. The **Automation** tab can queue a link for a niche. The **Library** shows each video's niche and TikTok account, and can show one niche at a time.
- Your current channel list and searches move into your first niche. If you never make a niche, nothing changes.
- On the command line: `ytclip niches`, and `ytclip auto --add LINK --niche NAME`.

### Clean up
**🧹 Clean up…** (in the Automation and Library tabs) clears out what nothing needs any more:
- unfinished downloads;
- preview renders;
- downloaded videos;
- saved transcripts and analysis;
- clips the quality check rejected;
- clips you've posted, and clips you removed in TikTok Manager.

Before anything goes, it shows every file and how much space it frees. You can untick whole kinds or single files, or send everything to the Recycle Bin instead.

Some things always stay:
- clips TikTok Manager still has waiting;
- anything for a video that's queued, being clipped or in the Studio's list;
- anything changed in the last 10 minutes;
- anything that isn't ClipMonster's own.

**Do this by itself once a day** runs it while automation runs. It clears the kinds you ticked, and only things older than the age you set (3 days to start with, never less than a day). On the command line, `ytclip clean` shows what would go, and `--delete` clears it.

### Honest bottom text
- Bottom text such as "Follow for part 2" now only shows on a clip that another clip carries straight on from. A part 2 that the quality check rejects doesn't count, because it never gets posted.
- With AI polish on, every other clip gets a line the AI wrote for it, such as a question about what happens. The AI's lines never promise a part 2, and they keep any @handle in your bottom text.
- Without the AI, those clips show **Bottom text when there's no part 2** (Settings → Layout). It's "Follow for more" to start with. To always use it, turn off **AI writes the bottom text when there's no part 2** (Settings → AI).
- Bottom text that doesn't promise a part 2, such as "Follow @you", stays the same on every clip. So does text you type for one clip in the Layout tab.

## Good to know
- TikTok Manager picks up every niche's folder when it watches the Automation folder. It doesn't sort clips by account yet, so check which account each clip is queued for.
- Renaming a niche sends new clips to its new folder. Clips already made stay where they are.

## Updating
Installed copies offer this update automatically. Close ClipMonster's other windows first. Automation pauses for the update and starts again afterwards.
