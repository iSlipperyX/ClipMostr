ClipMonster 3.3.0 adds the **Video Creator**: an original, faceless video from an idea, made start to finish on your PC. The local AI writes the script, a neural voice reads it, a picture is made and slowly moved for every scene, music and sound effects are mixed in, and word-by-word captions are burnt on. It goes to its category's account like your clips, labelled AI-generated. 3.2.0 wasn't released on its own, so its changes are below too.

## New: the Video Creator
- **The Creator tab.** Type an idea ("3 stoic habits that will change your life") or paste your own script, pick the category, the voice (▶ plays a sample), the length and the look. **Write storyboard** shows the script scene by scene (what's said, the picture, the camera move, the sound) and you can change any of it before **Make video**. Or **Add to automation queue**, and automation makes it in turn with your clips.
- **Scripts that hold attention, honestly.** A hook of at most 10 words (under 3.5 seconds), then narration at a narrator's pace, written to the length you asked for and asked again when it misses. No promised part 2, no made-up statistics stated as fact, no real people or text in the pictures. Each script is compared with the ones you've made before, and one that repeats an earlier one's wording is written again.
- **Today's facts, looked up online.** Before the script is written, the topic is searched on Google News, Bing News and Wikipedia, and the newest stories and today's date go to the AI, so "the latest GTA 6 news" is the latest, not what the AI remembered from training. The sources are listed with the video. *Settings → Creator → Look it up online first* turns it off.
- **Over a minute for Creator Rewards.** *Over a minute* keeps the video 62 to 85 seconds: the voice is read a little faster or slower to land on the length, and it learns each voice's real pace for the next script.
- **Eight narrator voices** with Kokoro-82M (natural; downloads about 340 MB the first time), falling back to Piper, Windows' own voices or eSpeak NG. Whisper lines every caption word up with the voice.
- **Pictures** from ComfyUI when it's running on this PC (or your own workflow), SDXL-Turbo or FLUX.1-schnell from Python, your B-roll folder matched by name, or built-in moody textures. Each scene falls back on its own, so one failed picture never stops a video.
- **Movement and sound.** Slow push-ins, pull-outs and pans on every picture, varied dissolves between scenes timed just before the words, whooshes and risers on the cuts, a music bed that ducks under the voice, mixed to −14 LUFS with peaks under −1 dBTP. Music comes from your music folder or is composed for the mood; sound effects are built in or your own. Only use music and sounds you have the rights to.
- **Six looks**: Cinematic dark, Documentary, Old-world painting, Horror, Bright and bold, Calm and dreamy, each with its own picture style, colours, music mood and caption colours. Picked from the category when you don't choose.
- **Checked before it's posted**: 1080×1920, sound, loudness, length and size. The folder gets `clips.json` (with the caption and hashtags posting uses), `storyboard.json`, a `README.md` on how it was made, the scene pictures and a thumbnail. The Automation tab lists made videos with ✨.
- **Settings → Creator** has every choice: script writer, voice and engine, length, look, pictures, ComfyUI, camera movement, scene changes, music and its level, sound effects, caption font and size.

## Command line
- `ytclip create "Dark psychology secrets" --category Psychology --voice deep_male --creator-rewards`
- `ytclip create --file prompts.txt --category DailyFacts --schedule` queues a video per line for automation.
- `--script FILE` uses your narration, `--storyboard-only` prints the storyboard (JSON) and `--storyboard FILE` makes an edited one. `--check` says what's ready on this PC, `--list-voices` lists the voices.

## From 3.2.0

ClipMonster 3.2.0 calls niches categories and ties each one to a TikTok account, turns Research into a command center, runs much bigger AI models on your own PC, and keeps going when the graphics card or a big upload gives trouble.

### New
- **Niches are now Categories.** The tab, the settings and the command line all say Category. Everything from 3.1 moves over by itself the first time 3.2 starts, and your old settings are kept as `settings.before-3.2.json`.
- **Every category posts to an account.** A new category is made together with the TikTok account it posts to, and one account can take several categories. A category without an account is held: nothing is picked or clipped for it, and the Categories tab and the health check say so.
- **Add account wizard.** Link an account you already have, or make a new one: TikTok's own sign-up page opens in that account's own browser profile, the wizard ticks off the steps, and the window closes once you're signed in.
- **A sign-in check every 6 hours.** Each account's sign-in is checked on TikTok Studio's upload page. When one has run out, a Windows notification's **Sign in again** button opens the wizard for that account.
- **Research is a command center.**
  - **Three views** of the same finds: an opportunity **Matrix**, the **Table**, and **Live arbitrage**: videos under 3 days old that are taking off right now and that you haven't clipped. (ClipMonster can't search TikTok, so it can't tell whether someone else has.)
  - Each video gets a **Viral potential** score from 0 to 100 (how fast it's gaining views, how sharp its Most replayed peak is, likes and comments per view, and how crowded its topic is), a **Momentum** reading (views an hour lately against its last day) and a **Lifespan** (gone in a couple of days, or evergreen).
  - A **momentum heatmap** of each topic hour by hour, a card per topic with its views sparkline, **outliers** flagged at three times their topic's usual, category pills with counts, and a menu on the table's header to choose its columns.
  - The **inspector** shows a video's Most replayed graph with the likely clips shaded, its punchlines and drop-offs, and what its viral potential is made of.
  - Performance's top videos get a **vs usual** column that flags the posts doing far better than the rest.
- **Bigger AI on your own PC.** *Local AI on this PC* now picks from four sizes by your graphics card's memory: Llama 3.2 3B (any PC), Qwen2.5 7B (8 GB), Mistral Nemo 12B (11 to 20 GB) and Qwen2.5 32B (24 GB and more). On your 16 GB RX 9060 XT that's Mistral Nemo 12B, all of it on the card, with room for 32,768 tokens of transcript at once.
  - A model too big for the card still runs, with the layers that don't fit in RAM (slower). Each start sizes the run to the memory that's free right then, and part of the card always stays free for Windows and your other programs.
  - Command R 32B is in the list too, but it's never picked for you: its licence doesn't allow commercial use.
  - The answers carry more: a headline, a hook rating from 1.0 to 10.0, the pacing (a *Dramatic Push-in* clip gets the slow push-in), what makes the moment work, and whether it's a Creator Rewards moment (one that's under a minute is lengthened past 62 seconds, to the end of a sentence).
  - 3.1's model keeps working until you download the better one: *Pick for this PC* never starts a download of several GB in the middle of a run. `ytclip local-ai download` gets it.

### Fixed
- **A graphics card failing part-way no longer stops the run** (a driver timeout, "device lost", running out of memory):
  - **Whisper** types the video out again on the CPU, with every thread. whisper.cpp going quiet on the card for 10 minutes (longer for a long video) counts as a failure too.
  - **The local AI** asks the same question again on the CPU.

  Either way the card is left alone for half an hour, `automation.log` gets the details, and *Settings → Speed* and the health check say when it's tried again. After three failures in a row Whisper stays on the CPU until you press *Try the graphics card again*. When Whisper runs out of graphics memory because the local AI's model is filling the card, the local AI is stopped to make room (it starts again when it's next needed).
- **Big uploads.** The wait for TikTok to take a video was a fixed 15 minutes. Now it follows TikTok's own upload progress: a big clip on a slow connection gets as long as it keeps moving, an upload stuck at the same percentage for 4 minutes stops with the reason, and a clip over 100 MB is only posted once TikTok says the upload is done.
- **The local AI's log says where it really runs.** 3.1 could say "the CPU" for a model running on the card.
- faster-whisper on an NVIDIA card whose CUDA libraries are missing now carries on on the CPU instead of failing the video.
- Checked with new tests: captions, karaoke highlights, bleeps and B-roll stay in step with the picture and sound through dead-air and filler cuts (to within a frame at 25, 29.97, 30 and 60 fps), and captions and banners burn in from folders with any name, brackets, commas, semicolons, percent signs and apostrophes included.

### Command line
- `ytclip categories` and `--category` (`ytclip niches` and `--niche` still work).
- `ytclip local-ai` lists every model with its size, its licence and how it would run on this PC: how many layers on the card, and how much transcript it can hold.
