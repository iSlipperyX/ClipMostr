ClipMonster 2.1 shows your clips exactly as they'll export while they play, makes every clip come out at TikTok's size by default, and adds one-click **top/bottom views** for the Layout tab.

## What's new

### Export view: see the finished clip while it plays
The preview now plays the selected clip as the export will look, frame for frame:
- The format and reframe, the blurred fill, bars and banner text.
- The stacked second video, playing in sync with your clip (and its sound, if you've turned it up).
- Captions that follow the speech word by word, with the spoken word highlighted, and the title box.
- **Safe zone** shades where TikTok's tabs, buttons and caption will cover the video.
- Drag the picture left or right to reframe it.

It's drawn from the same numbers the export uses, and it's tested against real exports pixel by pixel, so text, banners and videos land in the same place in both. Press `V` or click **Export view** to switch back to the full source video.

### Text that matches everywhere
- Line breaks are worked out by ClipMonster from the font's own measurements, so captions and banners break the same way in the preview, the Layout tab and the export, and long lines are evened out instead of leaving one word dangling.
- **Text sizes now mean letter height**, so size 45 looks the same in Montserrat, Anton or Bebas Neue. Your saved sizes are converted automatically and look exactly as before.
- Each clip can now hide a banner's words (**No text** next to the clip's banner text), keeping the bar.
- Still frames from the middle of a clip now show the same caption grouping as the export.

### TikTok size by default
- New clips export as **9:16 at 1080×1920**, ready to upload. The Export tab shows the exact output size.
- While **TikTok-ready output** is on, the frame is always 9:16. *Original* and *1:1* are fitted in with a blurred fill, and the size stays between 540×960 and 1080×1920 whatever resolution is picked.
- The built-in presets are all 9:16 now. *Square posts* switches TikTok-ready off, since 1:1 is for Instagram and X.
- To keep the original frame, turn TikTok-ready off in Export, or use `--no-tiktok` on the command line.

### Top/bottom view presets
A **View** picker at the top of the Layout tab:
- **Clip only**
- **Title on top**
- **Title top + follow bottom**
- **Text over video**
- **Meme (white bar)**
- **Gameplay below**
- **Gameplay below + title**
- **Gameplay above**
- **Big clip, small gameplay**

Built-in views keep the second video you've already chosen, and ask for one if you haven't.

- **Save…** stores your current layout as your own view, including the banners, the second video, fonts and colours. **Delete** removes one of your saved views.
- On the command line: `--layout "Gameplay below"`, and `--list-layouts` to see every view.
- Stacked videos now always fill their half of the frame edge to edge.

## Updating
Installed copies offer this update automatically. Your settings, presets and library carry over.
