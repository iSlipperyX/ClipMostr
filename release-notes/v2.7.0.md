ClipMonster 2.7.0 adds standing requests: Automation finds the videos you describe, every few hours, on its own.

## New
- **Standing requests.** Give a niche the videos you want, one request per line, in your own words. For example: *Call of Duty MW4 with good comedy*. Find it in the Niches tab, under Creative Commons searches.
  - **How often it runs.** Automation runs each request every 6 hours. Change it in Settings → Picking → *Run standing requests every*. **Find videos now** on a niche runs its requests straight away.
  - **What it looks for.** It searches YouTube the way Research's **Find videos** does, among videos posted in the last month.
  - **What it picks.** Videos that match 60 out of 100 or better are picked, under the same rules and limits as everything else: the niche's videos a day, the channel limit, blocked channels and words, and the minimum clip-ability. A video is never picked twice.
  - **Permission.** A video found for your request counts as permission to clip, like a link you add yourself. Its pick says "Found for your request (90 match)". If you only pick Creative Commons videos, that still holds.
  - **The log.** Automation's log says what each request found. When good matches aren't picked, it says why, for example "the niche's videos a day are reached".
  - **AI.** With a Claude or Gemini key, the AI writes the searches and rates the matches. That's two calls a run, counted in *AI calls per day*. Once the day's calls are used up, requests match by their words instead.
  - **Quota.** Each run of a request costs about 200 of the YouTube API's 10,000 free daily units.
- **Without niches.** Standing requests go in Settings → Picking. Making your first niche moves them into it.

## Updating
Installed copies offer this update automatically.
