ClipMonster 2.5.1 does two things:
- It makes the clip length you set a hard rule. No clip comes out shorter than **Min clip length**, whether it was made in the Studio, sent from Research, or made by Automation.
- Research can find videos for a request you type.

## New
- **Find videos.** Type what you want at the top of the Research tab, in your own words, for example *Call of Duty MW4 with good comedy*, and press **Find**.
  - **What it searches.** ClipMonster searches YouTube for it, such as "Call of Duty MW4 funny moments" and then "Call of Duty MW4". With an Anthropic key, Claude writes the searches instead. It knows MW4 is Modern Warfare 4.
  - **Match score.** Every video gets a **Match** score out of 100 for how well it is what you asked for. With a key, Claude rates each video. Without one, the request's words in the title, tags and description decide.
  - **What's left out.** Videos about something else, such as another game, are left out, and so are Shorts.
  - **Order.** The list goes mostly by Match, then by clip-ability.
  - **Time limit.** The menu next to the box limits results to this week, this month or this year.
  - **Getting back.** **← Back to trending** returns to the charts. Your last request stays one click away, and the box suggests your earlier ones.
  - **Cost.** A request uses about 200 of the YouTube API's 10,000 free daily units.
- **Queue in Automation** on the Research tab, for found videos and trending ones alike. Automation clips them next, each in its niche's style and with your clip length.
- **`ytclip find "your request"`** does the same from the command line. `--queue N` adds the best N to Automation, and `--niche` says which niche they're for.

## Fixed
- **Automation made 15 to 20 second clips even when you'd set a longer minimum.** Automation's style preset ("Shorts / TikTok / Reels" by default) carried its own clip length, 15 to 58 seconds, and that replaced yours. Clip length now always comes from Settings → Clip finding → Min/Max clip length. A niche's own clip length, or a clip request's, still goes on top.
- **Clips could come out up to a quarter shorter than the minimum.** The clip finder, Trim dead air and Part 2s each let a clip go down to 75% of Min clip length, so a 20-second minimum gave 15-second clips. Now a clip that comes out short grows to the end of a later sentence, or back to the start of an earlier one, within Max clip length and without running into the clips beside it. A clip with no room to grow is dropped, and the log says so.
- **Picking a preset in the Studio changed your clip length.** Presets now leave clip length alone and only change the look and the clip finder.
- **Min clip length can't be set above Max clip length any more.** Raise the minimum past the maximum, and the maximum follows it (the same the other way round).

Clips you edited by hand stay exactly as you made them. A video shorter than Min clip length still gives one clip of the whole video.

## Changed
- Each video's line in the Automation log now says the clip length it's clipped with, for example "clips 62–90 s".

## Tip for TikTok
TikTok's Creator Rewards count videos **longer** than one minute, so set Min clip length to 62 seconds or so, not exactly 60. Give Max clip length some room above that (90 seconds, say), so clips can end where a sentence ends.
