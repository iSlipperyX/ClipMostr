ClipMonster 2.0 adds two big pieces: a **Layout editor** for building TikTok-style frames, and a **Research tab** that finds trending videos worth clipping.

## What's new

### Layout editor
A new **Layout** tab next to Clips, Find clips and Export.
- **Text banners** above and below the clip, with a live to-scale sketch of the frame and a real-frame preview.
- **Stacked second video**: put gameplay, satisfying footage or anything else under (or over) the clip, with its own volume (0 keeps it silent).
- **Fit the clip** choice for how the clip fills its part of the frame.

### 13 bundled fonts
Anton, Archivo Black, Bangers, Bebas Neue, Lilita One, Luckiest Guy, Montserrat (Black, ExtraBold), Permanent Marker, Poppins (Black, ExtraBold), TikTok Sans Black and Titan One ship with the app, so captions and banners look the same on every PC without installing anything.

### TikTok-ready output (on by default)
Settings → Export → **TikTok-ready output**:
- Keeps captions and banners out of the areas TikTok covers with its buttons and caption text (in 9:16).
- Caps frame rate at 60 fps and bitrate at 20 Mbps, and uses 44.1 kHz audio.
- Warns about clips longer than 10 minutes or bigger than 287 MB.

### Research tab 🔥
Shows what's trending on YouTube right now, per country and category, and gives each video a **clip-ability score from 0 to 100** so you can see what's worth clipping at a glance.
- Pick regions (e.g. US, GB, CA), a category, and how many videos to pull per region.
- The best candidates get their *Most replayed* graph checked too.
- Can keep checking in the background while ClipMonster is open, and flags new high scorers.
- Also on the command line: `ytclip research -r US,GB -c gaming --top 15`.
- **Needs a free YouTube Data API key**, from the Google Cloud console. Paste it in Settings → Research (or set `YOUTUBE_API_KEY`). Each region checked uses 1 of the 10,000 free daily units.

## Updating
Installed copies offer this update automatically. Your settings and library carry over.
