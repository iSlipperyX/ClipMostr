ClipMonster 3.4.0 makes the Video Creator's pictures and facts better and lets you see its videos before your followers do: AI pictures on your own graphics card in one click, a fact check of every script against what was found online, scripts that learn from how your earlier videos did, and Creator videos that wait for your OK before they post.

## New
- **AI pictures, one click.** The Creator tab's **Set up AI pictures** link (or `ytclip create --setup-pictures`) builds a picture engine for this PC: its own Python, PyTorch with **DirectML** for AMD and Intel cards (CUDA for NVIDIA), diffusers, and Stability AI's **SDXL-Turbo**, then draws a test picture. About 10 GB and 10 to 30 minutes, once; after that every scene's picture is drawn on your card (576×1024), offline. On your RX 9060 XT that's DirectML. If half precision draws black pictures on a card, it switches to full precision by itself, and a scene it can't draw still falls back to your B-roll or a texture. SDXL-Turbo's [Community License](https://stability.ai/license) allows videos that earn money while you make under $1M a year.
- **Fact check.** After the script is written, the AI reads it again against the sources it looked up: every date, number, name and quote is confirmed, rewritten when a source says otherwise, or softened when nobody confirmed a specific number or date. Scene numbers in the storyboard get ✓ (fixed) or ⚠ (check it yourself), with what the source said on hover, and the video's README lists each change with its source. *Settings → Creator → Fact check*: fix, mark only, or off.
- **The Creator learns from your results.** Once four Creator videos have results, the script writer sees the titles and hooks that did best and worst on the account (views against its usual), and learns from how they open without copying them. Until then it sees your clips' hooks. The Automation tab's *What's working* compares Creator videos with clips.
- **Check videos before they post.** For an account's first two weeks of Creator videos, each one waits in the queue (⏸ **Needs your OK**) until you watch it and press **OK to post** in the Queue tab. *Settings → Creator → Check videos before they post* makes it always or never; *Post now* counts as your OK, and clips never wait.

## Command line
- `ytclip create --setup-pictures` sets up AI pictures; `ytclip create --check` shows the picture engine.
