ClipMonster 3.1.0 edits clips like an editor would, can find them with an AI that runs on your own PC, and has a new look.

## New
- **A new look.** A calmer, pro-app design in the spirit of Apple's and Adobe's desktop apps: neutral graphite surfaces, one blue accent, rounded corners, and line icons instead of emoji.
  - **Sidebar navigation.** The app's sections sit in a rail on the left, grouped into Create (Studio, Library, Research), Automate (Niches, Automation) and Publish (Accounts, Queue, Performance). New Research finds show as a count beside Research.
  - **Settings** has its sections down the left too, with each page under its own title.
  - **Light or dark.** *Settings → General → Appearance*: Dark (the default, best for judging video), Light, or Match Windows. It changes the next time ClipMonster starts.
- **Local AI.** *Settings → AI → Local AI on this PC* finds clips with a model that runs on your PC: free, private, no key, no internet once it's downloaded.
  - It picks the model for your PC: Mistral 7B on a graphics card with 8 GB or more (your RX 9060 XT runs it on the card), else Llama 3.2 3B. It downloads once, 2 to 4.4 GB, and a stopped download carries on.
  - Its answers are held to a strict format, so they're always readable: start, end, the hook, how strong the hook is (1 to 10), the payoff and the kind of moment.
  - With Claude or Gemini chosen, it takes over when they fail (once its model is downloaded).
  - `ytclip local-ai test` loads it and asks it to pick a clip, and the health check shows what it runs on.
- **Two more offline signals:** *Animated voice* and *Emotional words* score videos better with no AI at all.
- **Pro edit** (*Settings → Editing*, on in the Shorts / TikTok / Reels preset):
  - **Follow the speaker** with a smooth virtual camera that cuts to whoever's talking;
  - **punch-ins** on each new sentence, and a **slow push-in** on stories;
  - **dead air and filler words cut** ("um", "uh", "it was, *like*, huge"), with click-free joins;
  - **kinetic captions**: 1 to 3 words that pop in, the spoken word highlighted, money words green, danger red, questions yellow;
  - **bleeped swearing** in the sound, not just the captions;
  - **mastered sound** at −14 LUFS, TikTok's level;
  - **music** from your folder, picked by mood and ducked under the voice;
  - a **hook card** at the start and a **lower-third** that credits the source;
  - **B-roll** from your folder, matched to what's being said.
- **Creator Rewards.**
  - *Creator Rewards length* (and a preset for it) makes clips 62 to 88 seconds long, so they're over the minute TikTok pays for, even after the cuts.
  - The **Performance** tab shows each account's progress to 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in 30 days, with an estimate of when it gets there.

## Command line
- `--pro`, `--creator-rewards`, `--music FOLDER`, `--broll FOLDER` and `--ai local`.
- `ytclip local-ai [status|download|test]`.

## Also in this update: everything from 3.0.0

3.0.0 wasn't released on its own, so coming from 2.9.0 you get it now too. ClipMonster 3.0.0 is one app that does the whole job: it finds videos, clips and edits them, posts them to TikTok, reads how they do, keeps a record of each one and deletes its file once it's up. TikTok Manager is now part of ClipMonster, so there's one install and one set of updates.

### New
- **Posting is built in.** Automation's background process now posts too, so it keeps going with the window closed and starts with Windows. For every clip that passes the quality check, it:
  - **queues it for the right account.** Each niche picks the TikTok account it posts to;
  - **gets it ready.** The hook becomes the caption. Creative Commons clips get their credit line, and the AI writes hashtags fitted to the niche;
  - **posts it on schedule**, in TikTok Studio, through that account's own Edge or Chrome login;
  - **reads the profile** every 5 minutes for views, likes and followers, and spots posts you made from your phone.
- **A record of every posted video, then the file goes.** Once a post shows on the profile, a `.txt` and a `.json` go to `Documents\ClipMonster\Posted videos\<account>`. Each one has:
  - the post itself: when it went up, its link, the caption and hashtags;
  - the clip: its hook and length, and where in the YouTube video it starts and ends;
  - why it was made: the niche, the clip finder's score, the AI's rating and the quality check;
  - how it did, measured 1 hour, 24 hours and 7 days after posting.

  The clip, its captions and its thumbnail are then deleted. A post that never shows on the profile keeps its files, and its record says why.
- **New sections:**
  - **Accounts:** add an account and log in, set its schedule and hashtags, test posting, and set its profile picture, name and bio;
  - **Queue:** what each account posts next and when, and what was posted;
  - **Performance:** numbers, top videos and a live feed.
- **Notifications** pop up when a clip posts, a post fails, a login runs out or a video takes off.
- **First-run setup.** On first start, *Set up ClipMonster* walks through everything in about ten minutes:
  - the tools: Node.js, Firefox, and Edge or Chrome;
  - the AI and YouTube keys, with a test;
  - the YouTube sign-in;
  - your TikTok accounts and their logins;
  - a first niche;
  - the posting schedule;
  - Start with Windows.

  *Settings → General → Set up again* opens it any time.
- **A schedule based on posting research.** Each account starts at 2 posts a day, at least 3 hours apart. Post times shift by a few minutes each day, and new accounts warm up for two weeks.
- **A daily upload-page check** for each account, which catches a TikTok change before it fails a post.
- **The health check** now covers posting: accounts, logins, niches with no account, and a browser to post with.
- `ytclip posting` shows each account's login, what's waiting, the next post and what was posted.

### Removed
- **Google Drive and folder uploads.** Clips no longer need to travel anywhere: the PC that makes them posts them. Settings → Upload and `ytclip upload` are gone. A Google Drive sign-in from 2.x is no longer used: remove ClipMonster from your Google account's third-party access if you like.
- **The separate TikTok Manager app.** Uninstall it from Windows' Installed apps. Its accounts aren't carried over: add each one in the Accounts tab (or the setup) and log in once.
- **Clean up's** "Clips safely uploaded" kind. "Clips you've posted" now only catches posted clips whose files were kept, since posting deletes the rest itself.
