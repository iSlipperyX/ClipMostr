ClipMonster 3.12.0 hardens the Telegram bot and its Mini App: ask for a video in a **plain sentence**, **find a moment** in a game from your phone, **critique a video and approve v1 or v2**, and watch **one progress message** instead of a stream of them. Underneath, the bot keeps to Telegram's rate limits, retries what's safe to retry, and only listens to the people linked to it.

## New
- **Ask in a sentence.** *Make a 65s video about 3 dark psychology tricks for @PsychologyFacts*, or *Turn this link https://… into a reel for History*, in the chat or in the app's *Make* box. The category is the one you name ("for History", "to @account"); with none named, the one the idea clearly fits is used, and the reply says so with a **Not History? Change it** button; when nothing clearly fits you get the categories as buttons, each with its TikTok account.
- **A category's own voice, pictures and captions.** *Automation → Categories → Video Creator* sets them per category, and every video made for the category uses them unless the request says otherwise ("with a British voice", "karaoke captions"). Videos asked for from Telegram are upright 9:16 and kept between **62 and 85 seconds**: ask for 30 and you get 62, and the reply says why.
- **An article becomes a video.** A link to an article is read and retold by the Studio. A link to a video is still clipped; a bare link to a page asks which you meant.
- **Find a moment in a game.** `/find Mahomes 4th quarter touchdown` (or `/search`, or *Make → Sports highlights → Find a moment* in the app) searches a game's commentary and play-by-play. Each moment found comes back as a card with its time in the video, what was said and how sure the match is, with **🎬 Clip Play** and **➕ Add to Game Reel**; **🎞 Make the reel** makes one reel of the moments you set aside. Name the game with the moment ("red card in Arsenal Chelsea") or pick it from the buttons.
- **Critique / Regenerate, and two versions to choose between.** Every video the AI made carries **🛠 Critique / Regenerate**. Only what you name is made again: a caption or level change ("make the captions yellow", "the music is too loud") is one render of the video's timeline; another voice speaks the same script again; anything about the script or the pictures goes to the AI. The result comes back as **v2 (Critique Applied)**, in the chat and in the app. Neither version posts until you press **✅ Approve v1** or **✅ Approve v2**: that one is queued and the other is deleted from the PC.
- **One progress message.** While something you asked for is being made, the chat shows a single message, edited in place every 3 seconds: `⚙️ Working on: “Why Roman Concrete Lasted” [████████░░] 78% (Rendering Video)`. It survives a restart of automation and is taken away when the video arrives.
- **Mobile previews of big masters.** A master over Telegram's 50 MB is sent as a 720p preview (CRF 24, AAC 128 kb/s) marked **📱 Mobile Preview (1080p Master Saved on Host)**. The full-quality file stays in the posting folder and is what gets posted.

## Safer and steadier
- **Rate limits are kept, not just survived.** The bot paces itself to about one message a second per chat and under thirty a second overall. When Telegram still answers "too many requests", the wait it asks for is honoured for that chat, and a progress message simply skips a beat.
- **Retries that can't send twice.** A call Telegram failed on its side, or that got no answer, is tried again with a growing pause when repeating it is harmless (an edit, a status read). A message or a video is never sent a second time on a guess.
- **Uploads in pieces.** A video is streamed to Telegram from disk instead of being read into memory whole, and its progress shows on the progress message.
- **Only the linked people, in their own chats.** A message or button press is acted on only when it comes from a linked person in their private chat with the bot. Another person's tap on a forwarded button, a bot, a group or a channel is ignored and noted once an hour in automation's log. The Mini App refuses a sign-in for a bot account.
- **Floods aren't queued.** More than 30 messages or taps in 10 seconds from one chat get one "give me a few seconds" and are dropped, so the PC isn't handed a backlog. The Mini App's server limits each person to 40 requests that change something per 10 seconds, and limits sign-in attempts.
- **Malformed updates are dropped** instead of raising in the polling thread.

## Changed
- The bot's code is split by job: `bot.py` (dispatcher and polling), `studio_handler.py`, `sports_handler.py`, `review_pipeline.py`, `streamer.py`, `mini_app.py`, `state.py`. The old module names still import.
- The category buttons show each category's TikTok account, one category to a row.
- The button under a generated video reads **🛠 Critique / Regenerate** (it was *Say what to fix*).

## Good to know
- **A single play is under a minute.** *Clip Play* shows the clip's real length (a play runs 15 to 40 seconds), and a clipped moment waits for your OK rather than posting by itself, because Creator Rewards needs over a minute. A reel of three or more moments passes a minute.
- **A classic Video Creator video keeps no timeline**, so a caption change to one makes the same script again with the change, rather than a single render. Studio videos have a timeline and get the quick path.
- **The category guess is careful on purpose.** It only acts when one category clearly leads; otherwise it asks. If the small sentence model (MiniLM) is already on the PC it's used, and it is never downloaded just for this.
- The bot still runs on Python's standard library (threads and `urllib`), and the Mini App on `http.server`. Nothing new to install.
- League footage often gets copyright claims on TikTok.
