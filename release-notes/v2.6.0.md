ClipMonster 2.6.0 can use Google's Gemini as its AI, as well as Claude.

## New: Claude or Gemini (Settings → AI)
- **AI to use** picks Claude or Gemini for everything the AI does: the AI clip finder, AI polish (hooks, bottom lines, caption fixes, ratings, speaker crops), and the special requests on niches and clip requests.
- **Each AI has its own key.** Claude uses your Anthropic API key, as before. Gemini uses a Gemini API key, which you can make for free at [aistudio.google.com/apikey](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey). Keys can also come from the `GEMINI_API_KEY` (or `GOOGLE_API_KEY`) environment variable.
- **Each AI has its own models**, one for finding clips and one for polishing: Gemini 4 Argon (best), Gemini 3.8 Flash (fast, and the default for both) or Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (cheapest).
- **If it fails, try the other AI** (on by default). With both keys added, a video the chosen AI can't do (a rejected key, a used-up free quota, busy servers, or a refusal) goes to the other AI instead of falling back to smart signals. If the chosen AI has no key, the other one is used. The Automation log says when this happens.
- **Test keys** checks every key you've added, and that it can use both of the models you picked. It uses no tokens.
- The health check (Automation tab, `ytclip doctor`) checks the Gemini key too, and warns when the AI you chose has no key so the other one is being used.
- `ytclip --ai gemini` (or `--ai claude`) picks the AI for one command-line run.

Google may use what free Gemini keys send to improve its products; keys on a project with billing turned on aren't used that way.

## Updating
Installed copies offer this update automatically. Your settings are kept, and ClipMonster keeps using Claude until you choose Gemini in Settings → AI.
