## Clips in Drive keep their details

Each video folder's `clips.json` (ClipMonster's list of its clips: titles, scores, hooks, niche, part
series) now uploads to Google Drive or your upload folder too, just ahead of the clips. TikTok
Manager 1.9 can download your clips from Drive, on this PC or another one, and this is how they
arrive with their hooks as captions, in the right niche, and with "Part 1 / 2 / 3" kept in order.

- It isn't counted as a clip in the Automation tab's upload status.
- When it changes (a clip exported again, say), the copy in Drive is replaced, not doubled.
