ClipMonster 3.11.0 makes sports reels a matter of two taps: every game of the **last 24 hours** is listed, on the PC and in Telegram, and picking one makes its **recap reel**. What a Telegram request makes is **checked** before it's handed over, and the status says **which game is being cut** and how far along it is.

## New
- **The last 24 hours, in the Sports tab.** *Last 24 hours*, beside All / Picked / Ready, lists every game played in the last day from the public scoreboards (the NBA, WNBA, college basketball, the NFL, college football, the NHL, MLB and fifteen soccer leagues), with the score and whether it's over, being played or still to come. Narrow it with the **Sport** and **League** drop-downs and pick from the **Game** drop-down or the list. **Make its reel** does the rest hands-off; **Find its footage** only looks for the game, so you can tick its plays yourself. The same view is in ClipMonster for Mac under *Your PC → Sports*.
- **The same list in Telegram.** In the Mini App, *Make → Sports highlights → Pick a game* has the same drop-downs and list: tap a game, then **Make its reel**. In the chat, `/games` lists them with a button under each finished game (`/games NBA` for one league), and `/reel Lakers Celtics` asks in words. *Describe it* and `/sports` are still there for older games and for sports the scoreboards don't cover.
- **A reel is what you get.** A sports request from Telegram now makes one **recap reel of 65 to 85 seconds** unless you ask for single plays as well (*Reel and single plays*, *Single plays*, `/sports ... 3 clips`, `/sports ... both`). A reel made from only three plays is stretched to pass a minute too.
- **Checked before it's handed over.** Every video a Telegram request makes is measured: **over a minute**, **upright 9:16 at 720 x 1280 or more**, **with sound**, and it plays. One that meets them is queued as usual and the request says so. One that doesn't is kept but **waits for your OK**, with the reason beside it ("26 s (needs over 60 s)"), and never posts by itself. Asking for single plays says up front that they'll be held. *Settings → Telegram → Shortest video a Telegram request hands over* sets the length (61 s; 0 turns the check off).
- **You can see what the PC is cutting.** While the Sports worker is on a game, automation's status says which game, the step and the percent (*Sports: Finding the best plays of Lakers at Celtics: Downloading the game (40%)*, and how many jobs wait behind it), instead of only *Waiting: working on the games you picked in Sports*. The same line is at the top of the Sports tab, in the bot's `/status`, and in the Mini App with a progress bar (*On the PC now*).

## Better
- Each sports request in the Mini App shows the game's score, a progress bar while it's worked on, and what its check found.
- The reply to a sports request in the chat has a **Cancel it** button.
- A scoreboard that can't be read is named, and the other leagues are still listed.

## Good to know
- The games list comes from ESPN's public scoreboards, read from your PC, with no key. It covers soccer, basketball, American football, hockey and baseball; combat sports, cricket, motorsport and the rest are asked for by name, as before.
- A game still being played, or yet to start, is listed but can't be asked for until it's over: its highlights go up after it ends, usually within an hour or two.
- The check is for requests made from Telegram. A reel or clips you make yourself in the Sports tab are not held.
- League footage often gets copyright claims on TikTok.
